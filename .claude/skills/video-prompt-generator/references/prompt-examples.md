# Proven Video Prompt Examples

## Hook/Opening Scenes

### Attention-Grabbing Hook
```
A dramatic close-up of a person's face, eyes widening in surprise,
camera slowly pushing in, cinematic lighting with dramatic shadows,
photorealistic, high quality
```

### Tech Reveal Hook
```
Sleek smartphone floating in dark void, slowly rotating,
subtle blue light rays emanating from screen,
camera orbiting around device, 3D render style, premium feel
```

### Money/Finance Hook
```
Stack of hundred dollar bills falling in slow motion against black background,
camera tracking the falling money, golden light highlights,
cinematic depth of field, photorealistic
```

## B-Roll Examples

### Office/Work B-Roll
```
Close-up of hands typing on modern keyboard, soft natural light
from window, shallow depth of field, professional office setting,
camera slowly pulling back, realistic lighting
```

### Nature/Calm B-Roll
```
Peaceful forest scene with morning mist, sunlight filtering through trees,
slow camera pan from left to right, cinematic color grading,
calming atmosphere, 4K quality
```

### Tech/Digital B-Roll
```
Abstract digital particles forming network connections,
dark background with glowing blue nodes, camera flying through the network,
futuristic, data visualization style
```

### Urban/City B-Roll
```
Busy city street at golden hour, people walking in motion blur,
camera at street level slowly moving forward, cinematic warm tones,
shallow depth of field on foreground elements
```

## Camera Movement Prompts

### Push In
```
[Subject] in center frame, camera slowly pushing in toward face,
creating intensity, focus on eyes, smooth motion
```

### Pull Back
```
Close-up starting on [detail], camera pulling back to reveal
entire scene, smooth continuous motion, establishing shot
```

### Orbit/360
```
Camera orbiting around [subject] in smooth circular motion,
keeping subject centered, dramatic lighting, 360 degree view
```

### Drone Shot
```
Aerial view of [location], camera flying forward above the scene,
bird's eye perspective, epic scale, cinematic drone footage
```

### Tracking Shot
```
Camera following [subject] from the side as they walk,
matching their pace, eye level, documentary style movement
```
