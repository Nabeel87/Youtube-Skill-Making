# AI Video Tool Specifications

## Runway Gen-3 Alpha

| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| Max Duration | 5-10 seconds |
| Resolution | Up to 1080p |
| Aspect Ratios | 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3 |
| Strength | Best motion quality, realistic |
| Weakness | Short clips, expensive credits |
| Best For | Cinematic scenes, realistic footage |

### Prompt Tips for Runway
- Be specific about camera movements
- Describe lighting conditions
- Include style keywords: "cinematic", "photorealistic"
- Avoid complex multi-subject interactions
- Keep prompts under 300 characters for best results
