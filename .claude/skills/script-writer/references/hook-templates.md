# Hook Templates by Niche

High-converting opening hooks organized by niche and style.

---

## Tech & AI Niche

### Question Hooks
- "What if I told you there's an AI tool that can do [X] in seconds?"
- "Have you been using [Tool] wrong this whole time?"
- "Why are so many people switching from [A] to [B]?"

### Shocking Statement Hooks
- "This free AI tool just made [expensive software] completely obsolete."
- "I tested 50 AI tools so you don't have to - and the winner surprised me."
- "The tech industry doesn't want you to know about this."

### Promise Hooks
- "By the end of this video, you'll know exactly how to [achieve result]."
- "In the next 5 minutes, I'll show you [X] tools that will save you hours."
- "After watching this, you'll never [do thing the old way] again."

## Hook Formula Templates

### The Gap Hook
"Most people think [common belief]. But the truth is [unexpected reality]."

### The Transformation Hook
"I went from [bad state] to [good state] in [time]. Here's how."

### The Mistake Hook
"I made this mistake for [time] before I realized [lesson]."

### The Authority Hook
"After [experience/credentials], I've discovered [insight]."

### The Urgency Hook
"If you're still [doing thing], you need to stop right now."

### The Number Hook
"[Number] [things] that will [benefit] - number [X] is a game-changer."

## Hook Power Words

**Attention Grabbers:** Secret, Hidden, Revealed, Truth, Actually, Really
**Urgency:** Now, Today, Immediately, Stop, Before it's too late
**Curiosity:** Strange, Weird, Unusual, Surprising, Unexpected
**Value:** Free, Easy, Simple, Fast, Proven, Guaranteed
**Emotion:** Amazing, Incredible, Mind-blowing, Life-changing
