# CTA Templates

High-converting call-to-action scripts by goal type.

---

## Goal: Get Subscribers

### Soft CTA
"If you found this helpful, consider subscribing - I post videos like this every [frequency]."

### Value CTA
"I share [content type] every week that helps you [benefit]. Hit subscribe so you don't miss the next one."

### Challenge CTA
"I challenge you to subscribe and come back in 30 days to tell me your results."

### Community CTA
"Join the [number] others who've subscribed to become [identity/goal]. We're all on this journey together."

### Future Value CTA
"In my next video, I'm revealing [teaser]. Subscribe now so you don't miss it - it's going to be a game-changer."

---

## Goal: Drive Traffic (Website/Link)

### Direct CTA
"I've put together a free [resource] with everything we covered today. Link is in the description."

### Expanded Value CTA
"Want the complete guide with [extra stuff]? I've linked it below - it's free."

### Tool/Resource CTA
"The tool I mentioned is linked in the description. Use code [CODE] for [discount]."

### Lead Magnet CTA
"Grab my free [checklist/template/guide] - I've linked it below. It takes everything from this video and puts it in one place."

---

## Goal: Build Authority/Trust

### Experience CTA
"I've been doing this for [time] and I share what actually works. Stick around for more."

### Proof CTA
"These aren't theories - these are strategies that generated [specific result]. More proof coming in the next video."

### Consistency CTA
"I post [content type] every [frequency]. If you want to [achieve goal], this channel is your resource."

---

## Goal: Go Viral (Engagement)

### Comment CTA
"Drop a comment: which [tip/option] are you going to try first? I read every comment."

### Share CTA
"Know someone who needs to hear this? Send this video to them - it might change their life."

### Debate CTA
"Do you agree with my ranking? Let me know in the comments - I want to hear your take."

### Poll CTA
"Quick poll: [Option A] or [Option B]? Comment below - I'll share the results next week."

---

## Goal: Educate

### Action CTA
"Now that you know how to [skill], go try it right now. Then come back and tell me how it went."

### Next Step CTA
"This was step one. Watch [next video] to learn step two - I've linked it above."

### Practice CTA
"Pause the video and try this yourself. I'll wait... Okay, how did it go?"

### Resource CTA
"All the resources mentioned are in the description. Bookmark this video and come back to it."

---

## CTA Formulas

### The Value Recap CTA
"Today you learned [1], [2], and [3]. If you want more [content type], subscribe and I'll see you in the next one."

### The Question CTA
"Here's my question for you: [relevant question]? Drop your answer in the comments - I respond to everyone."

### The Double CTA
"If you made it this far, do two things: drop a comment with [X], and hit subscribe. That's how I know this helped."

### The Soft Sell CTA
"No pressure to subscribe, but if this video saved you time or money, it would mean a lot."

### The FOMO CTA
"My next video is going to be even better - I'm revealing [teaser]. Subscribe so you don't miss it."

---

## CTA Power Phrases

**Engagement:**
- "Let me know in the comments"
- "I read every single comment"
- "I reply to everyone"
- "Your feedback shapes future videos"

**Subscribe:**
- "Join the community"
- "Be part of the journey"
- "Don't miss out"
- "New videos every [frequency]"

**Action:**
- "Try this today"
- "Start right now"
- "Take the first step"
- "Put this into action"

**Share:**
- "Send this to someone who needs it"
- "Share with a friend"
- "Help someone out"

---

## CTA Mistakes to Avoid

❌ "Please like and subscribe" (too generic)
❌ Asking for too many things at once
❌ CTA at the very end only (should tease earlier)
❌ No specific reason to subscribe
❌ Forgetting to mention what value comes next
❌ Being too pushy or desperate

---

## CTA Timing

| Video Length | CTA Mentions | Placement |
|--------------|--------------|-----------|
| 1-3 min | 1 | End only |
| 5-8 min | 2 | Middle + End |
| 10-15 min | 2-3 | After hook, Middle, End |
| 20+ min | 3-4 | After value delivered |

**Rule:** Only ask for subscription AFTER delivering value.
