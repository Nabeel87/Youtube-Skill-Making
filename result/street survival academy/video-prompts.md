## ⚙️ TECHNICAL SETTINGS & SPECIFICATIONS

### Resolution & Quality
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 minimum (1080p)
- **Recommended:** 2560x1440 or 4K for downscaling quality
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (1.78:1)
- **Frame Rate:** 24fps for cinematic feel, 30fps acceptable
- **Color Space:** Rec.709 for web delivery

### Tool-Specific Parameters

**Runway Gen-3 Alpha:**
- Duration: 5-10 seconds per generation
- Style: Use "photorealistic" and "cinematic" keywords
- Camera movements: Specify clearly (push in, pan, static)
- Resolution: Generate at highest available, export 1080p minimum

**Kling AI:**
- Duration: Up to 10 seconds (strength for longer clips)
- Best for: Complex scenes with multiple elements
- Human motion: Kling excels at realistic human movement
- Environment: Strong at maintaining consistent backgrounds

**Luma Dream Machine:**
- Duration: 5 seconds per generation
- Best for: Motion graphics, simple animations, smooth movements
- Style: Clean, straightforward prompts work best
- Free option: Good for supplementary graphics

## 🎵 MUSIC & AUDIO RECOMMENDATIONS

### Background Music Style
**Primary Track:** Atmospheric crime documentary score
- BPM: 80-100 (moderate, not rushed)
- Mood: Serious, informative, slight tension
- Instruments: Ambient pads, subtle piano, electronic textures
- Volume: -18dB to -24dB (background, not competing with voice)

**Reference Style:** Netflix crime documentaries, "Making a Murderer," "The Staircase"

### Audio Layers
1. **Primary VO Track:** Clear, calm narration (center channel)
2. **Background Music:** Atmospheric score (stereo, low volume)
3. **Ambient Sound:** Urban environment sounds (traffic, footsteps) at -30dB
4. **Sound Effects:** Subtle whooshes for text animations, alarm sounds for case examples

### Sound Design Notes
- Keep audio clean and uncluttered
- No jarring sound effects
- Ambient city sounds add realism to outdoor scenes
- Silence is okay during key teaching moments
- Voiceover always prioritized over music

## 📐 COMPOSITION & FRAMING GUIDELINES

### Rule of Thirds
- Position key subjects on third-lines
- Avoid dead-center framing (except motion graphics)
- Leave appropriate headroom and looking space

### Safety Margins
- Keep critical text and subjects within 90% safe zone
- Account for mobile cropping and different displays
- Test on various screen sizes

### Text Overlay Guidelines
- **Font:** Bold sans-serif (Montserrat Bold, Bebas Neue, Impact)
- **Size:** Minimum 60pt for mobile readability
- **Contrast:** White text with black stroke, or black text with white stroke
- **Position:** Lower third preferred, never cover faces or key action
- **Duration:** On screen long enough to read 2x comfortably (minimum 3 seconds)
- **Animation:** Simple fade in/out, no distracting motion

---

## 🔄 TRANSITION RECOMMENDATIONS

### Between Major Sections (Between Signs)
- **Type:** 0.5 second cross-dissolve
- **Alternative:** Simple cut to black (1 frame) then next scene
- **Avoid:** Flashy transitions, wipes, star effects

### Within Same Section
- **Type:** Straight cut (no transition)
- **Match Cut:** When showing before/after, use match cut on position
- **J-Cut/L-Cut:** Overlap audio for smoother flow

### To Motion Graphics
- **Type:** 1 second fade to black, then fade in graphics
- **Alternative:** Smooth cross-dissolve
- **Purpose:** Clear mental separation between footage and graphics

## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR OPTIMAL RESULTS

### 1. Generate Multiple Takes
For each critical scene, generate 2-3 variations with slightly different prompts. AI video tools have variability - having options gives you the best final cut.

### 2. Extend Scene Duration
If a tool generates 5 seconds but you need 10, generate the scene twice with slight variation ("beginning of walk" vs "continuation of walk") and edit together.

### 3. Upscale If Needed
If any clips look soft, run through video upscaling AI (Topaz Video AI) before final export.

### 4. Consistency Is Key
For scenes showing the same location (like the parking garage used multiple times), generate all those clips in a batch using consistent prompt language to maintain visual continuity.

### 5. Motion Graphics Backup
If Luma doesn't generate clean graphics, consider using:
- Canva for simple text animations
- After Effects templates for motion graphics
- Keynote/PowerPoint export to video for simple slides

### 6. Security Footage Authenticity
To make Runway footage look more like real CCTV:
- Add timestamp overlay in post (free fonts available)
- Slight green or blue color tint
- 15-20% added grain/noise
- Slight fish-eye lens distortion in corners
- Lower frame rate (12-15fps) for that choppy CCTV feel

### 7. Render Order Efficiency
Generate scenes in this priority order:
1. Hook and key teaching moments (most critical)
